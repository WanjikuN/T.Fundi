# 1. Executive Summary

T.Fundi is a multi-tenant SaaS platform built to digitally transform the furniture industry.

It enables furniture businesses to showcase, customize, manufacture, sell, and deliver furniture through a single integrated platform while providing customers with immersive digital experiences such as 3D product customization, AI-assisted room visualization, and real-time manufacturing tracking.

Beyond commerce, T.Fundi aims to become the digital operating system for the furniture industry by supporting manufacturing operations, inventory management, analytics, learning, and future innovation.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

> To become the digital operating system powering the future of the furniture industry by enabling businesses to sell, manufacture, customize, and teach craftsmanship through modern technology.

---

# 3. Mission Statement

Our mission is to empower furniture businesses with technology that enhances craftsmanship, improves customer experience, simplifies operations, and creates opportunities for future artisans through education and innovation.

---

# 4. Product Purpose

T.Fundi exists to solve the disconnect between traditional furniture craftsmanship and modern digital commerce.

Customers should be able to confidently purchase customized furniture online while furniture businesses gain powerful tools to manage their operations from one platform.

---

# 5. The Problem

## Customer Challenges

Customers struggle to:

- Visualize furniture before purchasing.
- Know whether furniture fits their space.
- Customize furniture online.
- Trust expensive online purchases.
- Track production progress after placing an order.

---

## Business Challenges

Furniture businesses struggle with:

- Limited online visibility.
- Manual order management.
- Lack of manufacturing tracking.
- Inventory management.
- Customer communication.
- Fragmented business tools.

---

## Industry Challenges

The furniture industry lacks an integrated digital platform that combines commerce, manufacturing, customer engagement, and education into one ecosystem.

---

# 6. Our Solution

T.Fundi provides:

- Online furniture storefronts
- White-label business websites
- 3D furniture customization
- AI-powered room visualization
- Manufacturing workflow management
- Order tracking
- Customer communication
- Inventory management
- Analytics
- Future learning platform

T.Fundi will be developed as a production-grade software platform.

- Well documented
- Tested
- Secure
- Observable
- Maintainable
